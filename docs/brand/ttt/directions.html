O and X: three ways to draw oxbot

Black ground, O in warm white, X in signal red. Each direction shown as the app icon (180), phone (60), browser tab (32 and 16), and in the app. Pick one, or mix (e.g. A's icon with C's wordmark).

A · THE BOARD

A grid with one O and one X

The game, instantly. The two marks sit on the diagonal, so it reads as "a move in progress": the family and the bot taking turns.

B · THE PAIR

Just O and X, side by side

Boldest at small sizes and closest to the name. One short grid line between them keeps the game hint without the full board.

C · THE WORDMARK

The O and X are the letters

"oxbot" with the o drawn as an O ring and the x as a red X mark. Works for the website, the welcome screen and the title bar; the icon uses the pair.
