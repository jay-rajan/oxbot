Bots that run on a schedule, and moving your Grok bots in

You asked for a scheduler and for your Grok Bot bots in the main oxbot app, signing in when a task needs it. This is the design before code. It builds after step 2 lands and the new app is installed.

1 · Scheduler

What a person sees

  • Say it in plain words to any bot: "Every weekday at 8:15 AM, send me today's prep plan", "Every Saturday at 6 PM, check Divit's presentation", "Remind me Oct 20 to renew the car insurance". The bot answers with a schedule card: "Every weekday at 8:15 AM: today's prep plan. Create / Change / Don't". Nothing is scheduled without that yes.
  • Each bot's settings list its schedules (next run, last run, pause, delete). Settings → Schedules shows the whole home's, by person.
  • When a run finishes, its answer lands in the bot's chat and a notification goes to that person's devices: "Doordash prep: Day 3 plan is ready." A reminder is just a run whose task is the reminder text.
  • If a run needs an approval or a sign-in, it waits in the chat and notifies the person; it never acts unattended on anything that would ask.

Rules that keep it safe and cheap

  • Runs as the person who made it: their private bots, their Chrome profile, their AI spending. A shared bot's schedule belongs to its maker.
  • Counts against AI spending like any task; a run that would go over the cap is skipped and says so.
  • Missed runs (Mac asleep or oxbot closed): run once when oxbot is back if it's within 2 hours, otherwise skip and note it. oxbot can keep the Mac awake for a run (Settings, off by default).
  • Kid accounts: a parent approves each new schedule.
  • Limits: at most one run at a time per bot; at most every 15 minutes; up to 30 schedules per person.
  • Untrusted text never makes a schedule: a web page or email that says "schedule this" can't create one; only the person's own message, and only through the card.

  • Data (new migration): schedules(id, bot, person, task_text, rule, tz, next_run, last_run, paused, created_by_card, created_ms) and schedule_runs(schedule, started_ms, ended_ms, outcome, turn). Rules: once (date-time), daily, weekdays, weekly (days), monthly (day) at a time, in the home's time zone; stored as a small structured rule, not free cron text.
  • New bot tools (oxbot's own, through the gate): schedule_task (asks with a card), list_schedules, pause_schedule, delete_schedule (asks).
  • Scheduler loop in the hub: a one-minute tick; due runs become turns with origin "scheduled by <person>", so every existing rule (approvals, limits, private bots, untrusted text) applies unchanged.
  • UI: schedule card, bot-settings list, Settings → Schedules, notifications (the phone app already receives them).

Then the comparison

For each moved bot, 1–2 of your real past requests run in oxbot, graded against Grok's answer from the same chat: right / partly / wrong, what left the Mac, and the cost. Requests that would act (post, order, reply) stop at the approval request, which is the point.

3 · Order

What happens when

  1. Step 2 finishes (Claude and the old loop gone, plain words, Add to cart asks) and its tests pass.
  2. Back up, build, replace the installed oxbot; you sign in to OpenRouter.
  3. Build the Android app; you install and pair it.
  4. Recreate the 14 bots in your real oxbot (through the Assistant, so each one is a "create bot" card you approve).
  5. Build the scheduler (design above) and the "Shared with…" choice per bot; then add the routines.
  6. Run the comparison with Grok.
