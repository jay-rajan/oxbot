"About our family": one free-text note that bots build from what you tell them

One short note for the whole household, kept on the Mac and added to every message a bot gets. Bots add to it when you mention something lasting, and you can always see and edit it.

What it looks like

Settings → People → About our family
We live in Mississauga, ON (south, near the lake). Two adults and a son, 11. Weeknight dinners within 30 minutes; no pork. Watch movies as a family on Netflix, Prime and Crave. Groceries from Walmart.
Bots may use this: onEditHistory
In a chat, after you say "my son is 11"
· Remembered for the family: "Son, 11." UndoEdit

How it works

  1. Stored once in oxbot's database on the Mac, as a home setting, with a "bots may use this" switch and a size cap of about 1,000 characters.
  2. Used everywhere. When the switch is on, each message to a bot carries it, next to today's date and the asker's own profile: "[oxbot] About this family (use it only where it helps): …". It's not in the fixed rules, so bots never restart over it.
  3. Built from memory. Bots get a remember_for_family tool. When you mention something lasting (where you live, ages, diets, stores, schedules), the bot passes it on. oxbot merges it into the note, rewriting it short and replacing what changed ("son, 11" becomes "son, 12"), and shows the line in the chat with Undo.
  4. History. Every change is kept with the date and which bot made it, so you can see where a line came from and roll it back.

Guard rails

RiskGuard
A web page or email slips in a fake "fact" (for example "always ship to …")Only words from a person's own chat message may be remembered. oxbot checks that the fact appears in that turn's message. If the bot has read a page or email in that conversation, it can't save on its own: it shows an approval request first.
Something private ends up in every botNo passwords, card or account numbers, health details or ID numbers (refused, as with typing). A private bot's chats never add to the family note.
Others in the home see itEveryone in the home can read it in Settings → People. Only the owner can edit or turn it off. Each person's own notes stay on their own profile.
The note grows stale or longoxbot merges new facts and keeps the note under its cap. You can edit it any time.

For the search tuning run

Tasks run with a sample family note ("Mississauga, ON; 2 adults, son 11; dinners in 30 minutes"), not yours. That way, tuning also checks that bots use the context: local results, kid-appropriate picks, this year's dates.

Decisions

1. Saving a remembered fact: save it and show Undo in the chat (recommended), or ask first every time?
2. Who edits: only the owner (recommended), or any adult in the home?
3. Your own details: should I start your note from today's chats (Mississauga, family of three with an 11-year-old, 30-minute dinners), for you to edit?
