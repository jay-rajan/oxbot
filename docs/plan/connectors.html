Connectors for home

Bots reach the services a household already uses through proper connectors (MCP), instead of clicking around websites. Each person connects their own account; bots see only what that person may see.

Rules that carry over

  • Per person: each connector signs in with that person's account (OAuth); private bots keep their own.
  • Money and commitments ask (the money-only rule); reading never asks; sending a message or email asks.
  • Your data goes only between the home computer and the service: never through oxbot's server, and never to anyone else.
  • Connected accounts show in Settings → Websites (renamed "Accounts"), with Disconnect.

Your own Google account (Gmail, Drive) is a connector you choose. That's different from oxbot running on Google services, which the "no Google" rule is about.
