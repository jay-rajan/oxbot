oxbot · plan · Nov 9, 2026 · waiting for your OK

Better bots: they finish the job, and answer well

Two read-throughs of the code (main at e471738) found 24 problems. Most are not the model's fault: the bots are told to give up, ask for OK on harmless clicks, never see photos or PDFs, and get the weakest free model even for hard jobs. This plan measures first, then fixes, in six steps. Gets it done Answer quality

What's wrong today, in one picture

Decisions for you

  1. Escalation to paid models, inside your monthly limit, when a free model fails a step (recommended), or free only?
  2. The Assistant and research bots use a quality floor (likely a low-cost paid model) by default (recommended)?
  3. Real-model test runs cost money: OK to spend up to about $3 per full run of the task set, only when I ask first (recommended)?
  4. Personal filter: leave public details on web pages alone and add the on/off switch (recommended)?
  5. Unanswered cards in scheduled runs: skip that step, finish, and report (recommended), or wait longer?
  6. Your examples: 3–5 real tasks that went wrong would go into the task set first.
