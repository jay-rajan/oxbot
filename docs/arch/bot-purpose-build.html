Bot settings: what it's for

The bot's instructions now show under its name as "What it's for". Its maker (or the owner) edits them; everyone else who can open the bot reads them; a locked bot sends none. The Always allowed list is out of the window for now (the rules stay saved and still apply). Also in this round: Fine-grained control per website, and the cleanup.
